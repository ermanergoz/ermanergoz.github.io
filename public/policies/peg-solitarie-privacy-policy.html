Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2, 2026

This privacy policy describes how your information is handled when you use my application Peg Solitarie, and tells you about your privacy rights and the choices available to you. By using the service, you agree to the practices described in this privacy policy.

In short: I do not run my own servers and I do not personally collect, store or sell your personal data. The application shows optional rewarded advertisements provided by Google AdMob, and offers optional Google Play Games features; when you use those, Google collects certain data as described below. Your game progress is stored only on your device.

Collecting and Using Your Personal Data

Types of Data Collected

Data Collected by the Application Itself

The application itself does not collect, log or transmit any personal data or usage data to me. Game progress, settings, scores and saved games are stored locally on your device and never leave it, except through the optional Google services described below.

Data Collected by Third-Party Services

When you use the advertising or Play Games features described in the next sections, Google collects certain data, including your Advertising ID, IP address, device information and ad interaction data. This data is collected and processed by Google, not by me.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The application uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK (Google AdMob), a third-party advertising service provided by Google, to show rewarded advertisements. Rewarded ads are always optional: they are only shown when you explicitly choose to watch one in exchange for an in-game benefit, such as an extra hint or undo. The application never shows an ad without your action.

When an ad is requested, Google may collect and process data including:

  • Your device's Advertising ID (AAID)

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate your approximate location

  • Device information such as model, operating system version and screen properties

  • Ad interaction and diagnostic data, for example whether an ad was viewed or clicked

This data is collected and processed by Google, acting as a third-party ad technology provider, in accordance with its own privacy policy:

  • Google Privacy Policy

  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services

Depending on your consent choices, ads may be personalized or non-personalized. Non-personalized ads still use limited data, such as IP address and coarse location, for ad delivery, frequency capping and fraud prevention.

Your Ad Privacy Choices (Consent)

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the application shows a consent form (provided by Google's User Messaging Platform) before any ad is served, where you can consent to or decline the use of your data for advertising.

You can review or change your choice at any time: open the application and go to Settings, then Privacy, then "Manage ad consent". A link to this privacy policy is available in the same Settings section.

You can also reset or delete your device's Advertising ID at any time in your device settings, under Settings, Google, Ads (or Settings, Privacy, Ads, depending on your Android version).

Google Play Games Services (Optional)

The application offers optional integration with Google Play Games Services for sign-in, achievements, leaderboards and cloud-saved progress. If you choose to sign in, Google processes your Play Games profile (such as your gamer name and avatar), your scores and achievements, and your saved game data in accordance with the Google Privacy Policy linked above.

Signing in is entirely optional: every game feature except leaderboards, achievements and cloud save works without it. You can manage or delete your Play Games profile and data through your Google account settings.

Retention of Your Personal Data

I do not retain any of your data, because I never receive it. Data collected by Google for advertising or Play Games Services is retained by Google according to its own policies. Game progress stored on your device is deleted when you uninstall the application or clear its data.

Security of Your Personal Data

The security of your personal data is important to me, but remember that no method of transmission over the internet, or method of electronic storage is 100% secure. Since I do not collect or store your data on any server of mine, there is no developer-side database of your personal information. Data handled by Google is protected under Google's own security practices, which I cannot guarantee.

I do not knowingly collect or log any information from anyone under the age of 13, and ad serving is handled by Google in accordance with its policies. If you are a parent or guardian, and you are aware that your child has provided personal data through a third-party service used by the application, please contact me and the relevant service. If I become aware that personal data has been collected from anyone under the age of 13 without verification of parental consent, I take steps to have that information removed.
